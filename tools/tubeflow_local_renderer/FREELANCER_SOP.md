# Freelancer SOP: TubeFlow Local Renderer

## Before Starting Work

1. Double-click `Start_TubeFlow_Renderer.bat`.
2. Confirm the window says `Waiting for LosslessCut exports...`.
3. Keep this window open.

## For Each Video

1. Open TubeFlow.
2. Enter the title and steps.
3. Generate script and audio.
4. Download the audio.
5. Make sure the audio filename matches the video topic.
6. Start OBS recording.
7. Play the audio at `1.5x`.
8. Record the browser steps.
9. Stop OBS recording.
10. Open the recording in LosslessCut.
11. Trim the start delay.
12. Trim the ending delay.
13. Export to `Videos > LosslessCut`.
14. Wait until the renderer says `Final video ready`.
15. Open `Desktop > Ready_For_Google_Drive`.
16. Upload the `_FINAL.mp4` file to Google Drive.

## Do Not

- Do not rename the audio to a random name.
- Do not export the trimmed video to Downloads.
- Do not close the renderer while it says `Starting FFmpeg render...`.
- Do not upload the unmerged OBS recording to Google Drive.

## Ask Admin If

- It says `Audio not found`.
- It says `FFmpeg was not found`.
- The final video does not appear.
- The output video has wrong audio.
